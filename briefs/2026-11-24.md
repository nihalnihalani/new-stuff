## The one thing that matters most
**Machine-checked AI research went mainstream in a single day, and the common tool is Lean.** Anthropic published "VP ≠ VNP over every field" in its formal-math repo. It's a lower bound in algebraic complexity that an internal research Claude model found, and the README says the paper and its Lean 4 proof "were written by AI models, and no human mathematician has checked the proof". It doesn't settle P vs NP. Google DeepMind ran teams of Gemini 4 agents that met every four hours to compare ideas, and they proved every result in Lean. They cut the cost of a fusion-physics quantum circuit by 11.8×, a light-sensitive molecule simulation by 8× and arcsin, a common quantum subroutine, by 290×. DeepMind is open-sourcing the Lean library, problem specs and verified solutions. GPT-6.2 Astra produced an 1,800-line Lean proof of Smoktunowicz's gap theorem after 94 minutes and $21.58, according to Vals AI. When Mathlib lacked what the textbook proof needs, it found a different route. The pattern is the same each time: the model searches, and Lean decides what counts. For you, it means a weekend learning Lean and Mathlib is now a practical AI skill, not a niche maths hobby. ## AI news

### Security and agents
- **OpenAI and Cisco open-sourced Shirudo, a runtime for shielding Linux systems that can't be patched yet.** An agent writes a small eBPF program (a "shield") that blocks the exploit path while normal requests get through. Shields are signed, an operator has to approve them, and loading is logged with TPM-backed records. In a test, a Codex-generated shield stopped a Java4Shell exploit against a live Elasticsearch process without a restart. It's a real eBPF-plus-agents codebase you can study or contribute to. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-shirudo) · 02:50, 24 Nov · [@OpenAI](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2124980590717911527) · 03:07, 24 Nov · [repo](https://github.com/shirudo-dev/shirudo)
- **OpenAI says GPT-6 Cyber helped confirm 948 Linux kernel bugs that could grant local root access in one run**, and generated patches for review that were taken upstream with maintainers. AI bug-finding now produces work faster than maintainers can patch it, which is exactly the gap Shirudo is aimed at. [@OpenAI](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2125007927098699827) · 04:55, 24 Nov
- **Anthropic launched Claude Ship in beta: agents that review a PR, keep it green, and approve it if your policy allows.** PR Steward fixes failing CI, merge conflicts and review comments, but is designed never to approve or merge. Claude Approvals applies an approval policy written as a file in the repo. On the Claude Code team, merged PRs more than doubled from June to September while the share reverted fell by more than 40%. "Policy as a file in the repo" is a pattern worth copying in your own agent projects. [Claude](https://claude.com/resources/articles/introducing-claude-ship) · [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1040134/anthropic-claude-ship-ai-pull-requests) · 17:40, 23 Nov
