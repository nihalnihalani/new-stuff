## Build idea of the day
**Site voice-log agent for the Kaya AI Grand Challenge.** Construction site managers send voice notes about shortages and deliveries. SOV-Transcribe-1 turns them into clean text and keeps part numbers and phone numbers intact. Gemini 4 Hydrogen then turns that into structured purchase requests and flags urgent ones. Kaya requires you to name your models and report cost per run, and Hydrogen's flat $1.50/$7.50 pricing (free tier while prototyping) makes that easy to show. Score each request's urgency with Perplexity's open Decider Flash so you get calibrated probabilities instead of guesses.
