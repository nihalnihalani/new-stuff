## The one thing that matters most
**The Gemini Decisions API is a cheap classifier you don't have to train.** You ask up to 1,000 questions about text, images, audio, video or PDFs, and Gemini Decision 1 returns calibrated probabilities in about 150–250 ms. It costs $0.10 per 1M input tokens, output is free, and AI Studio has a no-cost tier for prototyping. For a student team, that means routing, grading, moderation and "which track does this fit" logic in one API call instead of a training run. The Decoder calls it part of a "decision-model wave". [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/) · 23:30, 11 Nov · [Sundar Pichai](https://x.com/sundarpichai/status/2120581155078729728) · 23:45, 11 Nov, 5,539 likes
