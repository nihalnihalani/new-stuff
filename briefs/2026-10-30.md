## The one that matters most
**Mistral released the open weights of Mistral Large 4 ("Le Chonk"), a model with about 1 trillion parameters built for agentic coding and cyber defense.** Mistral says it's #1 among open models on Cognition's FrontierCode, and you can download it from Hugging Face and run it on your own hardware. Artificial Analysis scores it 26 on its new index, which makes it the best open model built outside China by one point, though it still trails DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and GLM-5.3 by 6 points, mostly on coding. It also uses 39% fewer tokens per task than the preview did. 5,194 likes, and 1,252 points on Hacker News. [post](https://x.com/MistralAI/status/2115811242556137820) · 19:51, 29 Oct · [analysis](https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2115820547103952925) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50289418) · [weights](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B)
