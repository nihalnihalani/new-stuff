## The one that matters most
**OpenAI says its models disproved the Exponential Time Hypothesis, a decades-old belief about how hard SAT problems are, and checked the proof in Lean.** In the same release it claims a proof of the local smoothing conjecture in every dimension (which settles the Kakeya and restriction conjectures) and an existence proof for QCD that includes Yang–Mills, part of a Millennium Prize problem. ETH's original proposers, Impagliazzo and Paturi, plus Ryan Williams, are leading the outside check, and David Gross leads the physics group. If it holds up, hundreds of "this can't be done faster" results lose their footing. 576 points on Hacker News. [announcement](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/) · [ETH paper](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/paper.pdf)
