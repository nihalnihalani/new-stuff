## The one thing that matters most
**The era of cheap, unlimited frontier AI is tightening, so learn to run open models yourself.** Four signals landed in one day. First, OpenAI said a billing bug had given most users the wrong discounts on GPT-6.2, and limits now run out 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol (subscribers get an extra saved reset to make up for it). Second, micjm's battle data finds that GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol produce noticeably fewer reasoning and output tokens than their predecessors (p-values from 0.00013 to 0.022), which it reads as cost-tuning. Third, Nvidia told partners including Dell and Super Micro to pause server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia while it checks US export compliance. Fourth, Inc42 reports that Indian startups face GPU prices up 30–35% since February 2026 and are being pushed into 3–6 month rental commitments. Meanwhile local inference keeps getting better: llama.cpp v1.1.0 shipped, an AMD-specific fork claims about 55% faster generation, and "AI made self-hosting fun again" got 385 points on HN. If you can run a quantized open model on your own GPU, a price change or a rate limit can't stop your project. [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/gpt-6-2-astra-users-now-hit-limits-40-faster-after-openai-patched-a-pricing-error/) (26 Nov 22:58) · [micjm](https://micjm.com/blog/openai-is-in-cost-control-mode/) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50652527) (26 Nov 13:03) · [Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/memory-costs-become-a-headache-how-are-indian-startups-coping/) (26 Nov 19:50)

### Compute and pricing
- **Nvidia paused server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia.** According to the WSJ (via TechCrunch), Chinese firms had been renting Nvidia chips remotely from data centres in these countries until the US closed that loophole this month. The Institute for Progress estimates such sites supply about 24% of China's AI compute. Expect tighter GPU supply and higher cloud prices across Asia, India included. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/26/nvidia-hits-pause-on-chip-shipments-to-southeast-asia/) (27 Nov 01:33) · [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/nvidia-halts-server-shipments-to-malaysia-singapore-thailand-and-indonesia-after-tighter-us-export-controls/) (26 Nov 16:51)

Moonshot's cycle could double to 12 months, pushing Kimi K4 to mid-2027, and it is turning to Google and Meta models for training data now that OpenAI and Anthropic block distillation. The cheap Chinese open weights many student projects depend on may arrive more slowly. [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/after-openai-and-anthropic-shut-the-door-on-their-data-a-chinese-ai-developer-is-turning-to-google-and-meta-instead/) (26 Nov 21:15)

### India
- **BharatGen launched Patram 1.5, a 7B open-weight vision-language model built for Indian documents.** It reads a page as an image, so stamps, tables and handwriting aren't lost the way they are in plain OCR. You can ask for just the fields you need, in your own JSON format (BharatGen calls this "OCR 3.0"). It's trained to say when a field is blank or unreadable instead of guessing. It's Apache-2.0 on HF and a strong base for KYC, invoice or form apps. [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/bharatgen-launches-patram-1-5-ai-model-that-reads-understands-and-reasons-over-documents/articleshow/136194334.cms) (26 Nov 19:19) · [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/bharatgen-launches-patram-1-5-to-target-enterprise-document-processing/)

Whether a business shows up in AI answers is becoming something companies will go to court over. [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/indiamart-settles-legal-dispute-with-openai/) (26 Nov 18:48)

## Build idea of the day
