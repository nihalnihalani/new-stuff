# X Weekly Recap: 17 Oct to 24 Oct 2026 (01:30 IST)

**I read 123 posts from the 12 accounts and about 175 results from the 7 searches (the 6 on the watch list plus one for open hackathons), and kept 14 items.** All times are IST.

## The one thing that mattered most
**Open-weights models nearly caught the frontier this week.** First came DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, Reflection's Beam, Meta's Muse Glimmer, KRAFTON's Raon-1 mini, Ornith-1.5 and Recursive's Flint. Then Moonshot's Kimi K3.1 Pro arrived: on its own charts it is within a point of Opus 5.5 on Terminal-Bench 4.0, with weights promised by Nov 4. For hackathon builders, that means free models you can run yourself are now good enough for serious agent projects. [Kimi K3.1 Pro launch](https://x.com/Kimi_Moonshot/status/2113680701770068067) · 23 Oct 22:45

## Themes that held all week

### 1. Open-weights models kept coming, every few days
- Meta published the Muse Glimmer report: a 30B open model built for agents that runs on one consumer GPU at 4-bit. 1.4K likes. [post](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2112265822274302066) · 20 Oct 01:03
- Mistral launched Voxtral 3: transcription and speech models, plus a voice-agent SDK built on LiveKit. 3.3K likes. [post](https://x.com/MistralAI/status/2112574615318876178) · 20 Oct 21:30
- KRAFTON released a preview of Raon-1 mini, a 30B MoE with 4B active that it says matches Muse Glimmer on coding and agent tasks. 532 likes. [post](https://x.com/Krafton_AI/status/2112772568776339709) · 21 Oct 10:36
- Kimi K3.1 Pro launch: 15.7K likes, the biggest post of the week across all searches. [post](https://x.com/Kimi_Moonshot/status/2113680701770068067) · 23 Oct 22:45

**Where the argument went:** Early in the week, people said open models win only on price. By Friday they were asking whether Kimi's lead over Opus on some benchmarks is real. Every one of these scores is still self-reported.

### 2. AI is finding security bugs, and labs are tightening their own controls
- OpenAI said its models helped find critical security flaws in PHP, which runs about 70% of websites. 14.4K likes and 5.8M views, the top lab post of the week. [post](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2112907856018309586) · 21 Oct 19:34
- OpenAI released a Codex skill you can point at a repo to check whether your site is affected. 9.4K likes and 6.0K bookmarks. [post](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2112945805686604033) · 21 Oct 22:05
- Anthropic used Claude to formally verify software at large scale. 8.5K likes. [post](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2113390212911083894) · 23 Oct 03:31

**Where the argument went:** The week started with "AI finds bugs" and ended on Simon Willison's "vulnerability apocalypse" post ([post](https://x.com/simonw/status/2113633667583287786) · 23 Oct 19:38). The labs also turned to their own models. OpenAI brought in METR and Redwood Research to evaluate its internal monitoring ([post](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2113298833392496746) · 22 Oct 21:27) and later took live internet access away from models during training. Anthropic published a further analysis of the Claude Mythos 5 incident.

### 3. GitHub trending was all agent tooling
- The #1 repo of the week was rea, which reverse-engineers apps and binaries with agents. It gained 229K stars in one week. [post](https://x.com/trending_repos/status/2112153861129683421) · 19 Oct 17:38
- ai-job-search, a Claude Code framework that tailors CVs and preps interviews, was the daily #1 on two days. 312 bookmarks; it's directly useful for internship season. [post](https://x.com/trending_repos/status/2111791764684280137) · 18 Oct 17:39
- Cloudflare's security-audit-skill was the daily #1 on 22 and 23 Oct. It gained 2,979 stars in 24h on day one. [post](https://x.com/trending_repos/status/2113241275470066013) · 22 Oct 17:39

**Where the argument went:** Repo watchers kept saying models are becoming commodities and the value is moving to the layer between the model and your code: memory, context compression and orchestration ([post](https://x.com/likasanma/status/2112425079439257764) · 20 Oct 11:36).

### 4. Hackathon wins and Grok Bots
- A team won 1st place at the YC hackathon with D402, a way for AI agents to pay for things by doing work. 261 likes. [post](https://x.com/0xOptimus/status/2112252750469751097) · 20 Oct 00:11

- The Grok Bot marketplace works like an app store for ready-made bots, some built by X engineers. This roundup of favorites got 490 likes and 688 bookmarks. [post](https://x.com/itsolelehmann/status/2112189337299529966) · 19 Oct 19:59
- Brian Roemmele built a "Grok Bot Optimizer" that audits bloated personal bots. 275 likes. [post](https://x.com/BrianRoemmele/status/2112337968820334676) · 20 Oct 05:49

**Where the argument went:** Grok Bot posts moved from "look at my bot" to running whole teams of bots and keeping them tidy with auditor and optimizer bots.
