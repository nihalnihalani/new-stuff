## The one thing that matters most
**Gemini 4 Neon.** Google places Neon between Carbon and Hydrogen: close to frontier quality, but fast enough for agent loops. It costs $3 per million input tokens ($0.15 cached) and $15 per million output, with a 1M-token context. Google reports #3 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index (61, at xhigh thinking), #2 on the Vals Index (70.9%), #1 on Legal Research Bench and Tax Agent Bench, and #2 for steerability on Agent Arena. It also claims its lowest fabrication rate yet in Antigravity testing. It's live today in the Gemini API and AI Studio, Android Studio, Gemini for business, and for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app and Antigravity. For you, this is probably the sweet spot for hackathon agents. Carbon has been painfully slow (see Benchmarks), and Neon was served at 127 tokens a second on OpenRouter today, against 46 for Carbon.
