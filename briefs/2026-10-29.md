## The one that matters most
**Google DeepMind announced Gemini 4 Carbon, its new frontier model, and it's now #1 on Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 at 59, one point ahead of Claude Opus 5.5.** Google says it can work on long engineering tasks for days without getting stuck, and it leads on security benchmarks: 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro (finding bugs), 54.8% on ExploitGym and 71.0% on CWE-bench (patching). It also jumped 89 Elo to #4 on WebDev Arena. You can't use it yet: it goes first to a group of cyber defenders and trusted partners, then to developers "as soon as possible," at $2 in and $10 out per million tokens. It arrived two weeks after Gemini 4 Argon. 11,887 likes, and 836 points on Hacker News.
