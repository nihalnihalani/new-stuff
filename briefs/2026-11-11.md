## The one that matters most
**Frontier reasoning now costs about what a mid-tier model cost last month.** Google says Gemini 4 Carbon is #1 on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity's WANDR and Zapier's AutomationBench at "more than 2X lower cost per task" than the next best outside model, and it costs the same $2 in and $10 out per million tokens as Argon ([DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-4-carbon-is-now-available/) · 21:30, 10 Nov · [post](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2120219371473617099) · 23:47, 698 likes).
