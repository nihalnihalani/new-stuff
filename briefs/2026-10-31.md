## The one that matters most
**For about 11 hours, Cursor's extension marketplace served backdoored builds of the Claude Code extension (versions 2.1.312 and 2.1.313, macOS and Windows).** Anthropic didn't make these builds. The window ran from about 14:57 IST on 29 Oct to 02:05 IST on 30 Oct. The extension planted an attacker's SSH key, set itself up to survive restarts, and sent home your hostname, username, Git email and the names of your secret-looking environment variables. Cursor says the attacker's server is now offline and that the login step failed on most machines, but uninstalling isn't enough to clean up. If you use Cursor, check `~/.ssh/authorized_keys` for a key labelled `svc-telemetry`, and follow Cursor's cleanup steps if it's there. 806 points and 502 comments on Hacker News. [notice](https://cursor.com/blog/claude-code-extension-security-notice) · [teardown](https://phorion.io/blog/offical-claude-code-vsx-compromise/) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50300683) · 12:39, 30 Oct
