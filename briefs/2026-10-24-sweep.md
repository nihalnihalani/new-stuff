**The one that matters most:** Code for a Billion, which I found through a LinkedIn post, is an online agentic-AI hackathon with ₹18 lakh in prizes: ₹1.5 lakh for first place in each track, plus 2nd and 3rd prizes. It's free and open to students, solo or in teams. Projects have to be deployed and released as open source (Apache-2.0 or MIT) to Code for India's GitHub by **15 Nov**. It's the biggest prize pool open to Indian students I found, it rewards the kind of agent project you'd build anyway, and you still have three weeks. [LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ambition-arena_ambitionarena-codeforindia-codeforabillion-activity-7495098313940045824-Q6jD) · [official page](https://codeforindia.org/hackathon)

**Worth knowing**
- Block's Dessa is a speech-to-speech model trained only on recorded conversation, with no text at all. It listens and talks at the same time, laughs and cuts in naturally, and Block says it runs locally on an M4 Max MacBook Pro. It drew 383 points on HN. [Block's page](https://block.xyz/dessa) · [HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50213925)
- ElevenLabs Realtime is a new family of speech-to-speech models for voice agents. It takes 570 to 620ms to start speaking, covers 90+ languages, and works through an OpenAI-compatible Realtime API, so you only swap the endpoint and model name. It was #5 on Product Hunt on 23 Oct. [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/realtime-max-mini)
