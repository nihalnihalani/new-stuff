## Build idea of the day
**A hostel bill-splitter for the Global AI Builder's Challenge (deadline Sun 29 Nov, 04:30 IST), whose theme is making an everyday task easier.** Roommates snap receipts or upload PDF bills. markitdown turns the PDFs into text, and Cloudflare's Clairic Flash (9B, Apache-2.0) pulls typed fields out of each bill or photo: payer, date, total and line items. Clairic Flash reports a confidence score and null probability for each field, so the app asks a human to confirm only the low-confidence ones. That's a strong thing to show judges. Then show who owes whom in a simple web app.
