## The one thing that matters most
**GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol.** Astra is OpenAI's new frontier model, and Sol is the cheaper everyday model. OpenAI says Astra beats GPT-6 Astra on its Agents' Last Exam benchmark at under a third of the cost per task. It also claims Sol comes within 0.5 points of Astra on Cognition's FrontierCode 2.0 at about a fifth of the cost. In the API, Astra costs $8 per million input tokens, $0.40 cached and $40 output, while Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol's prices. For a student, the practical point is that Sol is now the sensible default for agent and coding work. Keep Astra for the rare hard task. Both are in ChatGPT and Codex for Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu users, and OpenAI is launching them with tighter cyber safeguards.
