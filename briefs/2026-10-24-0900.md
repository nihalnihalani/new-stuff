## The one that matters most
**OpenAI's red-teaming turned up a remote-code-execution bug in Brotli, the compression library inside Node.js, Bun and Envoy.** An attacker can take over an unpatched server or client just by sending it malicious compressed data, with no account needed. The fix has been in Brotli's source since February, but it was never shipped in a release. If your hackathon projects run on Node or Bun, watch for patched versions. 2.9K likes and 830 bookmarks. [post](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2113766354386084338) · 04:25
