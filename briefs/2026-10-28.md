## The one that matters most
**Google DeepMind announced Genie 4, a real-time world model that generates explorable, photorealistic scenes with native sound and characters who talk back.** You steer it with the keyboard and plain-text prompts ("change the weather", "add a person"), and Gemini 3.8 Flash runs in the loop to keep the scene coherent and track multi-step goals. DeepMind pitches it as a way to build training worlds for robots and agents, which makes it a natural hackathon toy once it opens up. For now it's a research preview for trusted testers. Next come Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie and early-access partners on the Gemini API and AI Studio, and there's a waitlist. It drew 16,855 likes, and the blog post got 775 points on Hacker News. [post](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2115114028338962672) · 21:40, 27 Oct · [blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation/)

## Labs and models
- Anthropic published "A 2^o(n) Time Algorithm for 3-SAT," credited only to Claude, a day after OpenAI's ETH claim. The paper says an internal Claude research model found the core algorithm on 16 Oct with 14M output tokens and no human input. So two labs now claim the same refutation, reached by different routes. 2,477 likes.
