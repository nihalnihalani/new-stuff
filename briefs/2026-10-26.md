## The one that matters most
**Nanbeige4.3-33B is out with open weights under Apache-2.0, and it's small enough to actually build on.** It's a 33B dense model with a 512K context, and on its own chart it beats Qwen3.8-27B on agentic coding (Terminal-Bench 2.1 80.9 vs 73.0, DeepSWE 65.5 vs 42.5), though Qwen still edges it on SWE-Bench Pro (61.7 vs 60.3). One post says people already run it 4-bit on a single 24GB card, and a distilled 3B version is next. For a hackathon, that's a coding-agent model you can self-host with a license that lets you ship. [post](https://x.com/YashChaudharyAI/status/2114398675506216970) · 22:18, 25 Oct · [model](https://huggingface.co/Nanbeige/Nanbeige4.3-33B)
